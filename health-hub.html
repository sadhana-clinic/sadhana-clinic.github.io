Preventive care

Building a preventive check-up habit

Most chronic conditions — diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders — develop quietly for years before symptoms appear. An annual check-up is the single highest-value thing most adults can do for their long-term health.

A good starting point: a general panel every year in your 20s and 30s, moving to a more complete package (like our Master Health Checkup) from your 40s onward, or sooner if a close family member has diabetes, heart disease or thyroid conditions.

What we look for first

  • Blood pressure
  • Blood sugar
  • Cholesterol
  • Kidney & liver function
  • Thyroid

Women's health

A guide to pregnancy check-ups

Regular antenatal visits track the health of both mother and baby — blood counts, blood group and Rh status, thyroid function, blood sugar, and screening for infections that need early treatment. Postnatal check-ups matter just as much, covering physical recovery alongside broader wellbeing.

Diabetes

Living well with diabetes

Diabetes management is a daily practice, not a single prescription. Consistent monitoring (FBS, PPBS and HbA1c), a realistic diet plan, regular activity, and nutritional counselling together do far more than medication alone.

We review kidney and liver function alongside blood sugar, since diabetes affects the whole body over time — that's why our Diabetic Health Check Up bundles these tests together.

Everyday habits that help most

  • Consistent meal timing
  • Regular activity
  • Home glucose checks
  • Scheduled HbA1c review

Healthy ageing

Senior health, simplified

After 60, the highest-value check-ups shift slightly: bone and calcium health, vitamin D and B12 levels, kidney and thyroid function, and cardiac screening all take on more weight. Our geriatric care evaluations are built around the pace and comfort our senior patients need.
