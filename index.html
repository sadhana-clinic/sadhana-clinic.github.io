Our story

From a neighbourhood practice to a trusted health partner

Sadhana Clinic was founded in 2008 by Dr. R. T. Selvi Prakash with a simple purpose: compassionate, ethical and reliable primary healthcare for the community. What began as a modest neighbourhood practice has grown into a trusted destination for families across Mahalakshmi Layout, Mahalakshmipuram, Saraswathipuram and Nandini Layout.

Our mission is to be a trusted family healthcare partner that promotes lifelong wellness — where every patient feels cared for, heard, and empowered to live a happier, healthier life.

Our vision is to be Bengaluru's most dependable full-service family clinic, delivering accurate, reliable and timely care to everyone who walks through our door.
