Our story

Compassionate care, rooted in one neighbourhood

Sadhana Clinic has spent 18 years earning the trust of families across Mahalakshmi Layout, Mahalakshmipuram, Saraswathipuram and Nandini Layout — one consultation at a time.

How it began

Sadhana Clinic was established in 2008 by Dr. R. T. Selvi Prakash, a family physician trained in the UK and Australia who returned to Bengaluru with one purpose: to bring compassionate, ethical and reliable primary healthcare to her own neighbourhood.

What started as a modest neighbourhood practice has, over 18 years, grown into a trusted healthcare centre serving families across four surrounding layouts — Mahalakshmi Layout, Mahalakshmipuram, Saraswathipuram and Nandini Layout in Bengaluru. Today the clinic combines personal attention with accurate diagnosis, having supported over 1 lakh patients through routine care, chronic disease management and preventive screening.

The clinic's growth has always followed the same principle it started with: care should be affordable, unhurried, and delivered by someone who remembers your history the next time you walk in.
