Founder & family physician

Dr. R. T. Selvi Prakash

Family Physician, Sadhana Clinic

Dr. R. T. Selvi Prakash founded Sadhana Clinic in 2008 after training in family medicine in the United Kingdom and infectious diseases in Australia. For 18 years she has built her practice around one idea: that primary care works best when it's personal, unhurried, and delivered by someone who knows your history.

  • MBBS — Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery
  • DFM — Diploma in Family Medicine, Royal College of General Practitioners (RCGP), United Kingdom
  • P.G. Diploma in Infectious Diseases — University of New South Wales (UNSW), Australia
  • 18 years in family practice, founder of Sadhana Clinic (est. 2008)
