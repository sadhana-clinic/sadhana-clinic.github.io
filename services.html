What we treat

Healthcare services for every life stage

From a first wellness check-up to managing a lifelong condition, our services are organised around how families actually need care — not around hospital departments.

General & Preventive Care

The foundation of everything we do. Regular check-ups and wellness visits catch small issues before they become big ones, and our immunisation and patient education programmes are built to keep whole families on track.

  • General health check-ups
  • Wellness visits
  • Immunisation / vaccination
  • Patient education
  • Asthma management
  • Diabetes care
  • Hypertension care
  • Thyroid management
  • Nutritional counselling

Disease Management

Chronic conditions need continuity, not one-off visits. We manage asthma, diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disorders over the long term, pairing medical treatment with practical nutritional counselling.

Women's Health

Care through every stage of a woman's life — from routine reproductive health screening to full pregnancy and postnatal support.

  • Pregnancy check-ups
  • Postnatal care
  • Reproductive health
  • Growth monitoring
  • Adolescent counselling

Child & Adolescent Health

Growth monitoring and counselling designed for the pace of childhood and the questions of adolescence — a steady presence as kids grow up.

Geriatric Care

Senior health evaluations that take the time older patients deserve — thorough, unhurried, and mindful of the conditions that become more common with age.

  • Senior health evaluation
  • Day care procedures
  • ECG
  • Laboratory investigations

Diagnostic & Support Services

Day-care procedures, ECG and a full laboratory panel — all available in-house, so a check-up and its results happen in one place.

Looking for a specific health package?

Our diagnostic packages bundle the right tests for check-ups, pregnancy, cardiac risk and more.
